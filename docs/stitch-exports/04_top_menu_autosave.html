Chapter 24

The Whispering Steel

The blade did not merely catch the moonlight; it seemed to consume it. Kaelen ran a calloused finger along the obsidian surface, feeling the unnatural chill that emanated from the core of the mountain's deepest forge. It was a weapon of silence, forged in a time when the stars were still young and the gods were still prone to walking the earth.

"It's heavier than it looks," a voice drifted from the shadows of the workshop. Master Thorne stepped into the dim light of the guttering candles, his eyes fixed on the artifact. "Not in weight of silver or iron, but in the weight of the stories it has ended. You carry a library of final words in that scabbard, boy."

Kaelen didn't look up. He could hear the faint hum—a vibration so low it was felt in the teeth rather than the ears. The obsidian wasn't just stone. It was a hungry void, waiting for a purpose, waiting for the first drop of blood to wake it from its thousand-year slumber.

Outside, the winds of the Northern Reach began to howl, battering against the stone walls of the citadel. The war was coming, not with the beat of drums, but with the creeping frost and the shadows that moved independently of their masters. Kaelen gripped the hilt tighter. The Digital Muse had guided his hand this far; now, the blade would have to do the rest.
