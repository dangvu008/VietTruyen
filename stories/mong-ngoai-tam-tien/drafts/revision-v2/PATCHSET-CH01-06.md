This patchset is the authoritative revision brief for the opening batch. No chapter in 1–6 may be accepted until the relevant fixes pass narrative-quality review.

## Ch.2 — Mười Bảy Năm

Blocking fixes:
1. Preserve 17 → 23 year verification, but show evidence through bridge/people/buildings instead of repeated declarative emphasis.
2. Lục Trầm must test at least one detail that the old woman rejects or cannot remember. This prevents every question from conveniently confirming him.
3. Keep his present age at 16. Do not create any record implying he was 14 only two Real-world months earlier unless the record is explicitly uncertain and the discrepancy itself is intentional.
4. End with three evidence classes only: people remember him; Hạ Hà changed independently; Dream/Real elapsed time does not match. Cause remains UNKNOWN.
5. Reduce `Không phải X / Mà là Y` rhetorical pattern and one-line paragraphs.

## Ch.3 — Chuyện Cũ Không Cũ

Blocking fixes:
1. Keep the three-notch wooden wedge as a strong object-memory anchor.
2. Item persistence must be written as an observation, not a universal law. Correct form: `objects previously obtained in Dream were present again on this return`; forbidden form: `Dream objects always persist`.
3. The table-mark experiment remains valid, but its result cannot be known until a later return.
4. Reduce aphoristic narration. Replace quote-like philosophy with practical behavior/notes.
5. Cổ Đạo light remains rumor only. No supernatural conclusion.
6. Tôn Thất may notice Lục Trầm, but Ch.3 should not add a second major revelation beyond `someone else remembers old events`.

## Ch.4 — Người Nhớ Chuyện Cũ

Blocking fixes:
1. Tôn Thất is not a clue dispenser. Reduce the number of independent clues he supplies.
2. He may provide: `Vạn Hòa`, `Tiền Phúc Sinh`, and `Bạch Thạch` as one causal memory cluster. He should NOT also conveniently solve Cổ Đạo, map routing, Dream similarity, and future clues in the same chapter.
3. At least one detail from Tôn Thất must be uncertain, incomplete, or later shown imprecise.
4. Lục Trầm must miss at least one useful question and realize it later.
5. Preserve Tôn Thất’s practical humor and merchant-road worldview.
6. Keep Cổ Đạo deaths mundane unless later evidence changes classification.

## Ch.5 — Cuốn Sổ Cũ

Blocking fixes:
1. Calendar prose must use forms such as `Thừa Bình năm thứ mười bốn, ngày hai mươi ba tháng tư`; never `Thừa Bình 14`.
2. Remove the age contradiction in the temporary-resident record. Preferred record: `Lục Trầm, nam, thiếu niên, không hộ tịch, tự xưng người Thanh Khê`.
3. Records establish that a person using the name Lục Trầm / Thanh Khê existed in the sequence; they do NOT prove objective identity with present Lục Trầm.
4. The protagonist must write `possible missing/unexplained interval`, not `I lost memory`.
5. Replace old ending `someone knew he would return` with the weaker message: `if you later meet a youth named Lục Trầm in Hạ Hà, give him this`.
6. Kỷ Tùng’s information is second-hand. Preserve provenance: unknown youth → Kỷ Tùng’s senior brother → Kỷ Tùng → Lục Trầm.
7. Five-notch wood is `UNVERIFIED_RELATION` to the three-notch wedge.

## Ch.6 — Một Nhánh Rêu

Core beat MUST be rewritten.

Forbidden sequence:
`Dream clue appears → Lục Trầm wakes → matching clue is immediately found in his family’s herb basket.`

Required sequence:
1. Kỷ Tùng shows a grey-silver moss sample and calls it `Ngân Ti` as a trade/local name, not confirmed taxonomy.
2. Lục Trầm remembers seeing something similar in Dream. Classification = `visual similarity only`.
3. He returns to Thanh Khê and deliberately searches: checks old herb scraps, asks father about damp-shade plants, asks where recent mountain batches came from, and initially finds either nothing or at least one false match.
4. Only after time/cost/action does he obtain a plausible similar sample from a known real-world source (e.g. a collector from Trúc Câu or a later incoming batch).
5. Similarity must remain unconfirmed. No claim that the same species exists in both worlds.
6. The chapter payoff is `a testable route / location to investigate`, not cross-world confirmation.
7. Keep family interaction and practical money humor so Reality has its own narrative value.

## Batch 1–6 rhythm repair

The old batch escalated central mystery almost every chapter. Revised rhythm target:

- Ch1: ordinary life + Dream anomaly.
- Ch2: verify elapsed-world change.
- Ch3: practical observation/experiment + human connection.
- Ch4: one witness cluster, incomplete information.
- Ch5: documentary verification, but identity remains unproven.
- Ch6: return to Reality and begin a slow practical search; no immediate cross-world payoff.
