# Long-form Concept Stress Test

Run before Story Bible approval for stories intended to exceed several hundred chapters.

Checkpoints: 1, 10, 30, 100, 300, 500, 1000.

At each checkpoint answer:
1. What does the protagonist want now?
2. How has the world expanded or deepened?
3. Where does the next conflict source come from?
4. What unused fuel remains in the central mechanic?
5. Why should the reader still continue?
6. What has changed besides raw power level?

FAIL when later checkpoints reduce mainly to stronger enemy → training → victory → new map, or when the central mechanic has only a few remaining variations.

PASS requires distinct character, social, knowledge, power and world progression, plus multiple renewable conflict sources.
