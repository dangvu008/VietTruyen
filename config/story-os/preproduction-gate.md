# Pre-Production Gate

TRUYEN must not jump from a vague request such as “hãy nghĩ một truyện hay” directly to Story Bible or prose.

## Mode A — Existing idea
Idea intake → concept validation → Story DNA → Story Bible → long-term outline → arc/volume → chapter pipeline.

## Mode B — Discovery / unfamiliar genre
Market Intelligence → Genre Research → Reference Analysis → Idea Mining → Concept Generation → Concept Evaluation → Red Team → Top 3–5 shortlist → Long-form Stress Test → approval → Story DNA → Story Bible → outline → writing.

## Fail-closed rules
- Current-market claims require fresh external research and dated sources.
- Reference analysis stores abstract craft patterns only; named plot beats, character substitutions, scene sequences and derivative imitation are forbidden.
- Red Team must identify reasons a concept may fail, including mechanic exhaustion, weak escalation, market mismatch, repetition risk and poor long-form fuel.
- A long-form concept cannot PASS if the 300/500/1000 checkpoint answers collapse into only “stronger enemy → train → win → new map”.
- Shortlist stage never auto-starts prose. Present 3–5 candidates with strengths, risks, novelty, audience fit and longevity; approval or deliberate hybridization comes first.
- Story DNA is the reader-experience contract. Story Bible is world/canon. Do not merge them.

## Authority
Pre-production research and concepts are not canon. They remain RESEARCH / PROPOSED / APPROVED_CONCEPT until Story Bible promotion explicitly occurs.
